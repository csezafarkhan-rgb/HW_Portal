# Why you can't find your products on wayfair.com

**Checked:** 26 Sep 2026 · live Partner Home and live wayfair.com · SUID 19440

---

## The short answer

Your products are **not missing**. They are highly visible, ranking at the top of search, and selling.

You cannot find them because **Wayfair does not sell them under your name.** Every product is listed
under one of Wayfair's own house brands. Searching "Home Weavers" on wayfair.com returns other
suppliers' rugs, because as far as the shopper is concerned, Home Weavers does not exist on the site.

## 2. Why you can't see them: Wayfair's house brands

The canonical URL of that same top seller is:

```
https://www.wayfair.com/bed-bath/pdp/winston-porter-w004083176.html
```

**Winston Porter** is a Wayfair house brand. The page carries no mention of Home Weavers anywhere.

Confirmed on a second range — the Essence bath rugs show on site as:

> **Ebern Designs** Bathroom Rugs, Extra Soft Absorbent Bath Rugs, Non-Slip, Quick Drying...

Wayfair renames the collections too:

| Your name | Name on site |
|---|---|
| Allure Collection | Dobrinka / Dobrinsky |
| Bruss Luxury | Luxury 100% Cotton |
| Essence | Ebern Designs |
| (supplier brand) Ess Ess Exports | Winston Porter, Ebern Designs |

This is normal for Wayfair's drop-ship model and is not an error — but it means **brand search will never
find you**, and it is the same mechanism behind the Allure → "Dobrinka" mis-shipment deductions we have
been disputing: customers receive packaging with a name that does not match the listing.

## What to actually do

1. **Stop looking for "Home Weavers" on wayfair.com.** Search the product name, or go via
   Partner Home → the product → View on site. Ask your RM which house brands your SUID is mapped to,
   and whether a supplier-branded storefront is available to you at all.
2. **Raise the house-brand naming with Ben**, alongside the reactivation ticket. The renaming is
   costing you money through mis-shipment deductions, not just recognition.
3. **Leave the ads alone for now** — both active campaigns are profitable. If anything, raise the
   daily cap on the one Wayfair flagged rather than starting new campaigns.
4. **Work the 1,034 live-zero-click parts.** That is where the upside is, and it is a content problem
   we can fix: images, titles, price.
