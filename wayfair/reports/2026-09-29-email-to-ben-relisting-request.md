The Containment Program FAQ, article 3731, states two things that matter here.
Relisting is a manual process and requests must be submitted by the SRM. Suppliers cannot submit
requests directly.
Paused products cannot recover automatically, because they generate no sales or post-order data,
so Wayfair cannot validate improvement without a manual review.

That means these parts will not return on their own at any point. The only route is a relisting
request submitted on our behalf, with a detailed improvement plan attached.

4. WHAT THE DATA ACTUALLY SHOWS

We can see 42 paused parts in the Customer Incidents and Returns Dashboard for the last six
months of delivery data. Expanded, each carries Pause, Castlegate Replenishment Restriction and
Safety Stock Restriction, all Enforced.

Across those 42 parts:
276 units delivered in total.
77 returns.
7 incidents.
36 of the 42 parts have a 0% incident rate - no damage, no defect, no missing parts, no mis-ship.
41 of the 42 delivered fewer than 30 units in six months. The median is 5 units.
6 parts were paused on one delivery and one return, which records as a 100% return rate.

So this is a returns problem, not a quality problem, and it sits on very small volumes.

One point we would ask the Containment team to consider.
Wayfair's WGS Containment programme does not raise a return based flag below 30 orders and 10
returns. Global Containment applies no minimum volume. Measured against Wayfair's own WGS floor,
41 of our 42 parts would not have qualified for a flag.
We are not asking for the rule to be set aside. We are asking for order volume to be weighed,
as Wayfair already does in its other programme.

5. ROOT CAUSE

We exported the Purchase Order and Photos Report covering 28 March 2025 to 23 September 2026 and
read all 2,106 customer return comments. The causes are expectation failures on the listing, not
manufacturing faults.

Root cause 1 - colour on site does not match the product. 803 of 2,106 comments, 38%.
Customers write "color looked pale", "color too orange", "rug looks white" against Ivory.
Linen is the worst colour at 269 returns, 12.8% of all our returns.
Six of our ten most returned parts are Linen.

Root cause 2 - size and fit are not clear before purchase. 306 comments, 15%.
Scale is not shown, so customers order a size that does not suit the room.

Root cause 3 - incomplete variant options on the listing. 28 incidents.
Wayfair's own records show "Option not Listed" in the Customer Received field, meaning the
variant shipped is not selectable on the listing. The customer then reports a wrong item.

Root cause 4 - set contents are not obvious. Open ticket WPI-371528 is a live example, where a
customer did not realise a lid cover was included in a four piece set.

Together, colour and size account for 53% of all returns.
Wayfair's own Containment guidance says buyer's remorse returns are preventable through accurate
descriptions, clear representative images, and correct dimensions and materials. That is exactly
the problem we have found, and exactly what we are fixing.
