# Email to Ben (Wayfair RM) — Containment relisting request + improvement plan

**Draft only — not sent.** Updated 29 Sep 2026 to follow Wayfair's own relisting procedure.

**Why it changed:** FIND-406710 was closed on 29 Sep. Wayfair confirmed the parts were paused by
the **Global Containment team**, US and CA, for high incidents and/or high returns. Per the
[Containment program FAQ](https://partners.wayfair.com/d/help-center/help/article/3731),
relisting is manual, **only an SRM can submit it**, it needs a **detailed improvement plan
addressing root causes**, and the Containment team judges it on whether the root causes are
clearly identified, the corrective actions are credible and measurable, and the plan is likely to
prevent recurrence.

So the email now has one job: get Ben to submit the relisting request, and hand him a plan he can
paste straight into the form. The covering note stays short; the plan is a separate block.

## Improvement plan — attach to the relisting form

```
IMPROVEMENT PLAN - HOME WEAVERS INC, SUID 19440
Listings: OPCO4702, FBWX1917, FBWX1956, FBWX2072
Prepared 29 September 2026

1. ROOT CAUSES IDENTIFIED

We exported the Purchase Order and Photos Report for 28 Mar 2025 to 23 Sep 2026 and read all
2,106 customer return comments. The causes are not manufacturing defects.

Root cause 1 - Colour on site does not match the product. 803 of 2,106 comments, 38%.
Typical comments: "color looked pale", "color too orange", "rug looks white" against Ivory.
Linen is the worst single colour at 269 returns, 12.8% of all our returns.
Six of our ten most returned parts are Linen.

Root cause 2 - Size and fit not clear before purchase. 306 comments, 15%.
Customers order a size that does not suit the space because scale is not shown.

Root cause 3 - Incomplete variant options on the listing. 28 incidents.
Wayfair's own records show "Option not Listed" in the Customer Received field, meaning the
variant delivered does not exist as a selectable option. Customers then report a wrong item.

Root cause 4 - Set contents not obvious. Open ticket WPI-371528 is an example, where a customer
did not realise a lid cover was included in a 4 piece set.

Incidents are not the driver. 36 of our 42 paused parts have a 0% incident rate.

2. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS - OWNER, DATE, MEASURE

Action 1. Re-shoot Linen, Ivory and Grey under neutral 5000K lighting, with a room reference
shot and a true colour swatch close-up on every part.
Owner: Zafar K. Complete by 31 October 2026.
Measure: colour related return comments on these colours fall below 20%, from 38% today.

Action 2. Extend the same re-shoot to all remaining colours across the four listings.
Owner: Zafar K. Complete by 30 November 2026.
Measure: buyer's remorse return rate on the four listings below the 5.23% class benchmark.

Action 3. Add a dimension callout overlay and an in-room scale image to every size variant.
Owner: Zafar K. Complete by 15 November 2026.
Measure: size related return comments fall below 8%, from 15% today.

Action 4. Audit and complete the variant option set on all four listings so every size and
colour we ship is selectable, and no shipped variant maps to "Option not Listed".
Owner: Zafar K. Complete by 31 October 2026.
Measure: zero new "Option not Listed" incidents from November 2026 onward.

Action 5. Add an explicit "what is in the set" image and bullet to every multi piece SKU.
Owner: Zafar K. Complete by 31 October 2026.
Measure: zero new missing component incidents on set SKUs.

Action 6. Answer every Wayfair product question ticket within 3 business days.
Owner: Zafar K. In effect immediately.
Measure: no product question ticket ages beyond 3 business days.

Action 7. Review the Customer Incidents and Returns Dashboard monthly, act on the worst parts,
and report progress to our Relationship Manager.
Owner: Zafar K. First review October 2026, monthly thereafter.
Measure: a written monthly review shared with Wayfair.

3. WHY THIS PREVENTS RECURRENCE

Wayfair's own Containment guidance states that buyer's remorse returns are preventable by
setting accurate customer expectations through accurate descriptions, clear representative
images, and correct dimensions and materials.
Our data shows 53% of returns are colour or size expectation failures, which is precisely what
that guidance addresses. Fixing the imagery attacks the actual cause rather than the symptom.

4. HOW PROGRESS CAN BE VERIFIED

Every measure above is visible to Wayfair in the CIRD without relying on our reporting:
incident rate, buyer's remorse return rate, and return comments per part, against the 1.70%
and 5.23% class benchmarks.
We are happy to be reviewed at any interval the Containment team prefers.

Zafar K.
Ecom Manager
Home Weavers Inc.
```
