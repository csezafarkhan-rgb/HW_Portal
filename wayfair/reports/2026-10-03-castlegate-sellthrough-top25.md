## Four findings

**1. Luxury never went into CastleGate.** No `CGBLUX` item exists in QuickBooks. The gap is
in the programme, not the data.

**2. CastleGate is still selling — it did not stop 3–4 years ago.** Waterford split almost
exactly in half: **1,072 sets in 2022–23 and 1,056 sets in 2024–26**. The best SKU,
`CGBWA4PC17212022GY`, actually accelerated — 107 sets then, **179 sets since**.

**3. Waterford is the programme.** 24 SKUs, 2,128 sets, 53% of the four collections'
revenue. The seven `CGBWA4PC17212022*` 4-piece sets alone are **756 sets and $37,683.69**.

**4. Bellflower sold volume at a loss.** 1,157 sets but only $10,957.71 — and **12 of its 18
SKUs carry negative gross margin**. Modesto, by contrast, has **zero** negative-margin SKUs
on similar volume. Worst cases below.

## Method

**Units are whole sets, not components.** QuickBooks reports each CG item as one sellable
unit. Proof: `CGBALL3PC172120GY` shows qty **64** with cost/unit **$15.84**, and $15.84 is
the *3-piece set's* purchase cost on the item record — 64 × $15.84 equals the total COGS
exactly. So it is 64 sets, not 192 pieces. The same check holds on
`CGBWA4PC17212022GY` (cost/unit $23.00, the 4-piece set cost) and on every set SKU here.

- Figures are QuickBooks' own Sales by Product values. Collection subtotals, rankings and
  unit sums are arithmetic on those values.
- The report tool caps rows, so Waterford was pulled in two windows (2022–23 and 2024–26)
  and added, plus a -$55.20 adjustment in 2021. That reconstruction reproduces QuickBooks'
  own full-period figure **exactly on all 11 Waterford SKUs where both were available**.
- The note asked for the "first 25" per collection. Every collection has **fewer than 25**
  CG SKUs, so each list above is complete.

### Data hygiene

`CGBBE1724GY.` (trailing full stop) and `CGBBE1724GY` are two QuickBooks items for what
looks like one product. The dotted one carries 84 units / $147.00; the other shows $86.57
with **zero** units. Together they misstate that colour. Worth merging.

### Still outstanding: what was SENT IN

This report answers *what sold*, in units and dollars, and *over what period*. It does not
yet answer **how much was sent into CastleGate**, because that is not in a sales report.

What is established: every CG item is an Inventory type, tracks quantity on hand, carries a
purchase cost, has an inventory start date between **May 2021 and June 2022**, and now shows
**QtyOnHand = 0**. So the receipts exist in QuickBooks as purchase/bill transactions against
these items and can be pulled — that is the one remaining step before sent-versus-sold and
true time-to-clear can be stated.
