## 1. What last year tells us

Wayfair's portal only exposes a full year of comparison at month level for the *current* month (the
date filter on Business Performance resets, and exports were blocked today), so this is built from the
year-on-year figures Wayfair does show, plus its own event calendar.

| August | 2026 | 2025 (implied by Wayfair's YoY) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $21,545 | ~$28,840 | **−25.3%** |
| Units | 1,362 | ~1,440 | −5.4% |
| Orders | 1,223 | ~1,323 | −7.6% |
| Customer visits | 9,085 | ~22,710 | **−60.0%** |
| Conversion | 6.13% | ~2.84% | **+116%** |

**Read:** the shop converts far better than last year, but barely a third of the visitors arrive.
Revenue fell faster than units, so the average selling price is down about 20% — the always-on
discounting is not buying volume, it is buying margin loss.

**By category (Aug YoY):** Bath Rugs & Mats −26.1% (96.7% of revenue), Area Rugs −47.9%,
**Bath Towels +360.7%** from a small base. Towels are the only growth line.

**Wayfair's Q4 calendar:** Way Day (submission closes 2 Oct), WFNA 5 Days of Deals and WFNA 48HR Sale
(both close **25 Sep**), Holiday Deal Week 12–19 Oct (already submitted), then Black Friday / Cyber
Week events which historically ran 1 Nov – 6 Dec for this account.

**Conclusion:** the gap is traffic, not conversion. Paid visibility on products that are in stock and
already convert is the fastest lever, and it should be live before the October events, not after.
