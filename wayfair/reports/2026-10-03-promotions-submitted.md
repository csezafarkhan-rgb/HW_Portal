## The discount rules applied

1. **$8.00 floor** — no post-discount cost may fall below $8.
2. Where the target discount would breach the floor, the rate steps down to a whole percent
   that leaves headroom rather than landing exactly on $8.
3. Items whose base cost is already under $8 cannot meet the floor at any rate, so they take a
   nominal **7% B2C / 8% B2B**.
4. **B2B is never shallower than B2C.**
5. Where the everyday B2B discount already matched or beat the promo, B2B was lifted to
   everyday + 2 so B2B buyers actually gain. At 22% this was needed on only 2 items; at 20% it
   was needed on 136.

## Two things worth remembering

**Wayfair's own recommendation was far deeper on the clearout** — 44–50% B2C and 50% B2B, against
our 18/20. On a clearance event curated by discount depth there is a real chance of being accepted
into the file but not surfaced. Worth checking after 13 Oct whether the clearout actually
delivered traffic, because that answers whether thin discounts on clearance events are worth
doing at all. The Way Day recommendation was much closer to what we submitted.

**Imports cannot be automated.** The Import Product Spreadsheet button uses the browser's native
file-picker API and never creates a file input in the page, so the Chrome extension cannot drive
it — verified three ways. Both files had to be imported by hand, from Firefox. Export, filling and
verification are all automatable; only the final import is not.
