### Caveat on method

These ranges are read from the lead image of each variant in the listing's media panel, not from
a full audit of every asset on every part. They establish that replacement has or has not begun
on a listing; they do not prove every one of the 10 images per part has been swapped. A full
check is only worth doing once the ticket closes.

The Change Log tab, which would show this directly, returns a **500 server error** on these
listings.
